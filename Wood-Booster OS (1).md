# Founder's Charter

## Founder

**Marc Järvinen**

---

# Why Wood-Booster OS Exists

Wood-Booster OS was not created because the world needed another business application.

It was created because skilled craftspeople spend too much of their lives fighting computers instead of creating meaningful work.

Technology should reduce complexity.

It should not create it.

Wood-Booster OS exists to return time, focus and knowledge back to its users.

---

# The Founder Vision

Every hour spent searching for files,
rewriting the same information,
moving data between applications,
or remembering forgotten details
is an hour taken away from craftsmanship.

Wood-Booster OS is designed to give that hour back.

---

# The Core Belief

Craftsmanship deserves software designed with the same care as craftsmanship itself.

A cabinet is not built by accident.

Neither should software.

Every screen.

Every button.

Every automation.

Every AI decision.

Every line of code.

Must have a purpose.

---

# The Philosophy

The computer exists to support the craftsman.

The craftsman does not exist to serve the computer.

---

# The Mission

Remove unnecessary work.

Preserve knowledge.

Increase creativity.

Automate repetition.

Support decisions.

Protect craftsmanship.

---

# The Long Vision

Wood-Booster OS is designed to live longer than today's technologies.

Programming languages will change.

Frameworks will change.

Artificial intelligence will change.

Hardware will change.

The philosophy will remain.

---

# The Role of Spacemonkey

Spacemonkey is not a chatbot.

Spacemonkey is not a mascot.

Spacemonkey is not an assistant.

Spacemonkey is the operating intelligence of Wood-Booster OS.

Its purpose is simple.

Understand.

Remember.

Protect.

Organize.

Learn.

Guide.

Never control.

Always assist.

---

# Every Feature Must Answer

Before any feature is accepted into Wood-Booster OS it must answer one question.

**Does this genuinely improve the user's work?**

If the answer is no,

it does not belong inside Wood-Booster OS.

---

# The Responsibility

Every developer working on Wood-Booster OS inherits a responsibility.

Protect simplicity.

Protect speed.

Protect the user's attention.

Protect the user's data.

Protect the original vision.

Technology may evolve.

The mission must not.

---

# The Promise

Wood-Booster OS will never measure success by the number of features.

It will measure success by the amount of time, knowledge and peace of mind it returns to its users.

---

# Legacy

Wood-Booster OS is not built for a software release.

It is built for decades.

It is intended to become part of the daily work of craftspeople, preserving knowledge and making it available to future generations.

The ultimate goal is not to create software.

The ultimate goal is to create a digital workshop where people can do their best work.
