============================================================
                     HEARTHWOOD — TRIBES LORE
============================================================

INTRODUCTION
------------
The tribes of Hearthwood are not simple factions. They are
manifestations of the primal forces that shaped the world:
Saplight, Hollow, Ember, and Stone. Each tribe carries a
philosophy, a culture, and a destiny tied to its origin.

Their alliances and conflicts define the balance of Hearthwood.

============================================================
SAPLIGHT TRIBE — THE BREATH OF THE FOREST
============================================================

ORIGIN
------
Saplight emerged from the first pulse of life that spread
through Hearthwood’s roots. When the forest breathed for the
first time, Saplight was born.

PHILOSOPHY
----------
“Life must grow, even through stone.”

Saplight believes growth is sacred. They heal the forest,
restore ruins, and protect the living memory of Hearthwood.

MAGIC
-----
Saplight magic is warm, green, and relentless. It heals,
strengthens, and binds allies together. Their druids channel
light through roots and moss.

COMMON UNITS
------------
Mosswarden — silent guardian of the deep roots
Saproot Channeler — druid who guides living energy
Thornback Sentinel — warrior clad in natural armor
Groveheart Titan — colossal being of bark and sap

ITEMS & RELICS
--------------
Mossdrop Vial — healing essence of moss
Saplight Knot — protective druidic charm
Groveheart Relic — symbol of ancient growth

RUNES
-----
Saplight Glyph — rune of renewal
Rootweave Rune — rune of living connection

RELATIONS
---------
Friendly with Stone Tribe
Wary of Ember Tribe
Distrusts Hollow Tribe
