HEARTHWOOD — VERGE CODEX (FULL EDITION)

============================================================
I. INTRODUCTION — THE HIDDEN FIFTH FORCE
============================================================
Verge is the primal force that exists between realities. It is
not creation, destruction, memory, or growth. It is the
threshold — the boundary where truth becomes possibility.

Verge is the force that:
- separates what is real from what is remembered
- divides timelines
- creates echoes before events occur
- allows beings to exist in multiple states
- rewrites identity when the world fractures

The tribes do not acknowledge Verge.
The forest does.

============================================================
II. THE NATURE OF VERGE
============================================================
Verge is not stable.
It pulses, folds, and distorts.

Its manifestations include:
- temporal loops
- memory storms
- reality splits
- echo distortions
- identity fractures

Verge is the only primal force that can:
- override runes
- rewrite relics
- alter tribal history
- change the outcome of past events

Verge is awakening.

============================================================
III. THE FIVE LAYERS OF VERGE
============================================================

1. **Threshold Layer**
The border between Saplight, Hollow, Ember, and Stone.

2. **Echo Verge**
A metaphysical plane where memories take physical form.

3. **Fracture Verge**
A zone created during the Great Fracture where time behaves
incorrectly.

4. **Seraph Verge**
The realm where Verge Seraphs once existed.

5. **Crownless Horizon**
The origin of all primal forces — a place without time, form,
or identity.

============================================================
IV. VERGE ENTITIES
============================================================

ECHO SERAPHS
------------
Origin: Prehistoric beings that existed only in memory.
Nature: Half-real, half-remembered.
Power: Timeline manipulation, echo creation.
Lore: Their disappearance fractured the world.

CROWNLESS HERALDS
-----------------
Origin: Chosen by Verge itself.
Nature: Beings who can walk between realities.
Power: Reality passage, memory rewriting.
Lore: The forest whispers their names.

MEMORY WEAVERS
--------------
Origin: Formed from Verge storms.
Nature: Entities that stitch memories together.
Power: Identity alteration, echo binding.
Lore: They appear during Echo Storms.

VERGE WRAITHS
-------------
Origin: Hollow spirits corrupted by Verge.
Nature: Echoes that exist in multiple timelines.
Power: Multi-state existence, temporal drift.
Lore: They are feared even by Hollow elders.

============================================================
V. VERGE RUNES — THE SCRIPT OF REALITY
============================================================

REALITY-SPLIT SIGIL
-------------------
Meaning: Division, parallel memory.
Power: Creates duplicate timelines.
Lore: Only Echo Heralds can perceive it.

ECHO-LOOP GLYPH
---------------
Meaning: Recursion, inevitability.
Power: Traps spells or beings in loops.
Lore: Hollow claims it is alive.

CROWNLESS MARK
--------------
Meaning: Threshold, awakening.
Power: Allows passage between realities.
Lore: The forest whispers this rune’s name.

VERGE-PULSE RUNE
----------------
Meaning: Instability, distortion.
Power: Causes temporal pulses and memory storms.
Lore: Appears during Fracture events.
