HEARTHWOOD — RUNES LORE (v2 EXPANDED EDITION)

============================================================
INTRODUCTION — THE LANGUAGE OF PRIMAL FORCES
============================================================
Runes are the oldest language in Hearthwood. They predate the
tribes, the primal entities, and even the forest itself. Runes
are not symbols carved into matter — they are *intent* given
shape.

Every rune belongs to one of the Fourfold Wilds:
Saplight, Hollow, Ember, Stone.
A hidden fifth category, Verge, has recently awakened.

Runes exist in three layers:
1. First Script — primal runes formed at creation.
2. Deep Script — runes formed during the Age of Roots.
3. Fractured Script — runes shattered during the Great Fracture.
4. Verge Script — runes that rewrite reality.

============================================================
SAPLIGHT RUNES — BREATH OF THE FOREST
============================================================

SAPLIGHT GLYPH (First Script)
Origin: Formed when the first pulse of life spread through the
roots of Hearthwood.
Meaning: Growth, renewal, healing.
Power: Restores vitality, strengthens allies, awakens roots.
Deep Lore: Saplight Glyphs “breathe” in rhythm with the forest.

ROOTWEAVE RUNE (Deep Script)
Origin: Woven into the first living roots.
Meaning: Connection, unity, living bonds.
Power: Binds allies, entangles foes.
Deep Lore: Tightens when danger approaches.

GROVE-MARK TABLET (Deep Script)
Origin: A rune carved by the forest itself.
Meaning: Territory, sanctuary.
Power: Creates zones of protection and rapid growth.
Deep Lore: Marks the forest’s domain.

VERDANT SPIRAL (Fractured Script)
Origin: Formed when Saplight collided with Stone during the
Great Fracture.
Meaning: Overgrowth, uncontrolled life.
Power: Causes rapid, chaotic growth.
Deep Lore: Verdant Spirals are feared even by Saplight druids.

============================================================
HOLLOW RUNES — ECHO OF WHAT WAS LOST
============================================================

SHADECOIL RUNE (First Script)
Origin: Formed from the first shadow that ever fell.
Meaning: Echo-binding, spectral tether.
Power: Binds spirits, amplifies echoes, weakens light.
Deep Lore: Contains “the memory of absence.”

HOLLOW-SCRIPT SIGIL (Deep Script)
Origin: Written by the Hollow Marsh during the first eclipse.
Meaning: Void, silence, forgetting.
Power: Erases magical traces, creates silence zones.
Deep Lore: Saplight druids fear it deeply.

ECHO-MARK TABLET (Deep Script)
Origin: Carved from shadow-infused stone.
Meaning: Memory, resonance.
Power: Reveals hidden echoes, amplifies spectral forces.
Deep Lore: Glows when spirits pass nearby.

VOID-THREAD GLYPH (Fractured Script)
Origin: Formed when Hollow magic split during the Fracture.
Meaning: Fragmentation, memory loss.
Power: Splits spells, erases fragments of identity.
Deep Lore: Hollow elders forbid its use.

============================================================
EMBER RUNES — ASH THAT REMAKES THE WORLD
============================================================

EMBERFLARE RUNE (First Script)
Origin: Born from the first ritual flame.
Meaning: Fire, ignition, transformation.
Power: Ignites flame, enhances Ember magic.
Deep Lore: Heats the air around it.

BLIGHT-SCRIPT SIGIL (Deep Script)
Origin: Formed when Ember and Toxic forces collided.
Meaning: Corruption, decay, mutation.
Power: Spreads blight, weakens healing.
Deep Lore: Forbidden in Saplight territory.

ASH-SCRIPT GLYPH (Deep Script)
Origin: Etched during ancient firestorms.
Meaning: Ash, memory of flame.
Power: Creates ash clouds, ember shields.
Deep Lore: Ignites when spoken aloud.

CINDER-FRACT RUNE (Fractured Script)
Origin: Formed when Ember magic shattered during the Fracture.
Meaning: Volatility, explosive change.
Power: Causes unstable flame bursts.
Deep Lore: Ember cultists worship it.

============================================================
STONE RUNES — MEMORY OF THE WORLD
============================================================

EARTHBIND GLYPH (First Script)
Origin: Carved into the first monolith.
Meaning: Stability, grounding, permanence.
Power: Stabilizes terrain, halts movement.
Deep Lore: Resonates with Stone Titans.

STONE-FRACT RUNE (Deep Script)
Origin: Formed when the Relic Spine cracked.
Meaning: Endurance, fracture, ancient memory.
Power: Creates shockwaves, reveals hidden runes.
Deep Lore: Used to awaken relics.

RUNEBREAK TABLET (Fractured Script)
Origin: A shattered tablet from the Age of Roots.
Meaning: Disruption, raw primal force.
Power: Breaks magical bindings, destabilizes runic fields.
Deep Lore: “Remembers too much.”

GRAVELIGHT MARK (Deep Script)
Origin: Carved from glowing relic stone.
Meaning: Revelation, vigilance.
Power: Reveals illusions, exposes Hollow echoes.
Deep Lore: Used by Stone sentinels.

============================================================
VERGE RUNES — THE BOUNDARY OF REALITY
============================================================
Verge runes are not carved.
They *appear* when reality destabilizes.

They belong to the hidden fifth primal force: Verge.

REALITY-SPLIT SIGIL (Verge Script)
Origin: Appears during temporal distortions.
Meaning: Division, parallel memory.
Power: Creates duplicate timelines.
Deep Lore: Only Echo Heralds can perceive it.

ECHO-LOOP GLYPH (Verge Script)
Origin: Formed when a memory repeats itself.
Meaning: Recursion, loop, inevitability.
Power: Traps spells or beings in loops.
Deep Lore: Hollow claims it is alive.

CROWNLESS MARK (Verge Script)
Origin: Appears in places where primal forces converge.
Meaning: Threshold, awakening.
Power: Allows passage between realities.
Deep Lore: The forest whispers this rune’s name.

============================================================
RUNE RELATIONSHIPS — META-LANGUAGE
============================================================
Some runes amplify each other:
- Saplight Glyph + Earthbind Glyph = Verdant Pulse
- Shadecoil Rune + Echo-Mark Tablet = Spectral Chorus
- Emberflare Rune + Ash-Script Glyph = Inferno Bloom
- Stone-Fract Rune + Runebreak Tablet = Monolith Shatter

Some runes cancel each other:
- Saplight Glyph cancels Blight-Script Sigil
- Hollow-Script Sigil cancels Emberflare Rune
- Earthbind Glyph cancels Cinder-Fract Rune

Verge runes override all others.
