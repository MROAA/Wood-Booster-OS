============================================================
                     HEARTHWOOD — ITEMS LORE
============================================================

INTRODUCTION
------------
Items in Hearthwood are personal artifacts infused with the
primal forces of Saplight, Hollow, Ember, and Stone. Unlike
relics, which shape the world, items shape individuals. They
carry memories, magic, and purpose.

============================================================
SAPLIGHT ITEMS — BREATH OF THE FOREST
============================================================

MOSSDROP VIAL
-------------
Origin: Distilled from the oldest mossbeds of the Green Veins.
Effect: Healing, regeneration, root restoration.
Lore: Mossdrop Vials are created only during the Blooming Moon.
Saplight druids claim each drop contains a fragment of the
forest’s breath.

SAPLIGHT KNOT
-------------
Origin: A woven charm made from living roots.
Effect: Protective ward, growth enhancement.
Lore: The Knot tightens when danger approaches. It is said to
be the forest’s way of warning its chosen.

GROVEWEAVE CHARM
----------------
Origin: Threads of bark and sap woven into a talisman.
Effect: Strengthens allies, weakens corruption.
Lore: Groveweave Charms hum softly when near Ember magic.

THORNWHISPER LOOP
-----------------
Origin: A ring of thorned bramble.
Effect: Piercing aura, defensive spikes.
Lore: The Loop whispers warnings to its bearer, though few
understand its language.

============================================================
HOLLOW ITEMS — ECHO OF WHAT WAS LOST
============================================================

WRAITHFANG CHARM
----------------
Origin: A fang taken from a spectral beast.
Effect: Echo strike, fear aura.
Lore: The fang vibrates when spirits draw near. Hollow ritualists
use it to locate forgotten souls.

NIGHTROOT FETISH
----------------
Origin: A ritual object carved from the roots of the Hollow Marsh.
Effect: Shadow enhancement, spectral binding.
Lore: Nightroot Fetishes are buried with the dead to preserve
their memories. Some return to the surface on their own.

SHADEGLASS VIAL
---------------
Origin: A vial forged from shadow-infused crystal.
Effect: Spectral mist, echo disruption.
Lore: Shadeglass is said to form only where Hollow and Ember
collide.

ECHO-COIL TOKEN
---------------
Origin: A twisted coil of spectral thread.
Effect: Echo duplication, illusion creation.
Lore: Echo-Coils allow the bearer to leave behind false memories.

============================================================
EMBER ITEMS — ASH THAT REMAKES THE WORLD
============================================================

EMBERROOT IDOL
---------------
Origin: A burning effigy carved from emberwood.
Effect: Flame burst, ash healing.
Lore: Emberroot Idols never cool. They burn with the memory of
ancient fire rituals.

BLIGHTROOT PHIAL
----------------
Origin: Toxic sap harvested from corrupted flora.
Effect: Poison burst, corruption spread.
Lore: Blightroot Phials are forbidden by Saplight druids, who
claim they “teach the forest to rot.”

CINDERBLOOM FLASK
-----------------
Origin: A flask containing living flame-flower essence.
Effect: Fire bloom, volatile healing.
Lore: Cinderbloom grows only in places touched by Ember Titans.

ASHBINDER CHARM
---------------
Origin: A charm forged from ritual ash.
Effect: Fire resistance, ash cloak.
Lore: Ashbinders are worn by Ember raiders during initiation.

============================================================
STONE ITEMS — MEMORY OF THE WORLD
============================================================

STONECOIL CHARM
---------------
Origin: A coil of stone carved with runes.
Effect: Binding aura, defensive fortification.
Lore: Stonecoil Charms are carved from monolith fragments and
carry echoes of ancient truths.

EARTHSHARD RELIQUARY
--------------------
Origin: A container made from relic stone.
Effect: Memory storage, rune amplification.
Lore: Earthshard Reliquaries store fragments of history. Some
contain memories no tribe wishes to recall.

RUNEBREAK TABLET
----------------
Origin: A cracked stone tablet etched with primal runes.
Effect: Rune burst, stability disruption.
Lore: Runebreak Tablets are dangerous. Stone elders forbid their
use, claiming they “remember too much.”

GRAVELIGHT PENDANT
------------------
Origin: A pendant carved from glowing relic stone.
Effect: Light pulse, hidden threat detection.
Lore: Gravelight Pendants reveal illusions and Hollow echoes.
