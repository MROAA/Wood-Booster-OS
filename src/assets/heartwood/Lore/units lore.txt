============================================================
                     HEARTHWOOD — UNITS LORE
============================================================

INTRODUCTION
------------
Units in Hearthwood are not simple warriors or creatures.
They are manifestations of the primal forces that shaped the
world: Saplight, Hollow, Ember, and Stone. Each unit carries
a story, a purpose, and a connection to its tribe.

============================================================
SAPLIGHT UNITS — BREATH OF THE FOREST
============================================================

MOSSWARDEN
----------
Origin: Born from the oldest mossbeds beneath the Green Veins.
Role: Silent guardian of the deep roots.
Abilities: Regeneration, root-binding, protective aura.
Lore: Mosswardens awaken only when the forest is threatened.
Their bodies are woven from living moss and ancient bark.

SAPROOT CHANNELER
-----------------
Origin: Druids who learned to speak with the roots.
Role: Healers and conduits of Saplight energy.
Abilities: Healing pulses, growth manipulation.
Lore: Channelers guide the forest’s breath, restoring allies
and binding enemies with living roots.

THORNBACK SENTINEL
------------------
Origin: Warriors grown from thorned bramble.
Role: Frontline defenders.
Abilities: Piercing strikes, thorn armor.
Lore: Sentinels are created, not born. Saplight druids weave
them from bramble and bark during sacred rites.

GROVEHEART TITAN
----------------
Origin: Manifestation of the forest’s collective will.
Role: Colossal protector.
Abilities: Tremor stomp, root eruption, healing aura.
Lore: Titans slumber for centuries. When they rise, entire
groves move with them.

============================================================
HOLLOW UNITS — ECHO OF WHAT WAS LOST
============================================================

HOLLOW STALKER
--------------
Origin: Formed from the first shadow that ever fell.
Role: Silent hunter of the marsh.
Abilities: Stealth, echo-strike, fear aura.
Lore: Stalkers leave no footprints. They are absence given form.

WRAITHCALLER
------------
Origin: Ritualists who bind spectral echoes.
Role: Summoners of spirits.
Abilities: Echo summon, spectral lash.
Lore: Wraithcallers hear voices no one else can. They command
spirits of forgotten beings.

SHADEBOUND REVENANT
-------------------
Origin: Souls that refused to fade.
Role: Undying frontline fighters.
Abilities: Shadow regeneration, echo shield.
Lore: Revenants are bound by Hollow sigils. They fight until
their memories finally dissolve.

ECHO-SPIRIT
-----------
Origin: Remnants of ancient souls.
Role: Support and disruption.
Abilities: Echo pulse, spectral drift.
Lore: Echo-Spirits drift through the Hollow Marsh, whispering
memories of the dead.

============================================================
EMBER UNITS — ASH THAT REMAKES THE WORLD
============================================================

CINDERBLOOM SHAMAN
------------------
Origin: Fire ritualists who mastered ember flora.
Role: Hybrid healer/damage caster.
Abilities: Flame bloom, ash mend.
Lore: Shamans wield flowers that burn eternally. Their magic
hurts and heals in the same breath.

ASHBORN RAIDER
--------------
Origin: Warriors forged in ritual flame.
Role: Aggressive frontline fighters.
Abilities: Burning strike, ash cloak.
Lore: Raiders are marked with ember sigils that glow in battle.

BLIGHTROOT CULTIST
------------------
Origin: Toxic alchemists corrupted by Ember’s twin force.
Role: Debuff and corruption specialist.
Abilities: Blight spores, toxic lash.
Lore: Cultists believe corruption is the purest form of change.

EMBER-SPIRIT
------------
Origin: Elementals born from living flame.
Role: High-speed attacker.
Abilities: Flame dash, ember burst.
Lore: Ember-Spirits flicker between worlds, leaving trails of
burning ash.

============================================================
STONE UNITS — MEMORY OF THE WORLD
============================================================

RUNBOUND GOLEM
--------------
Origin: Construct carved from monolith fragments.
Role: Heavy frontline tank.
Abilities: Rune shield, stone slam.
Lore: Golems carry runes older than language. They obey only
the Stone Tribe.

EARTHSHARD GUARDIAN
-------------------
Origin: Born from the Relic Spine’s deepest caverns.
Role: Protector of monoliths.
Abilities: Earthbind aura, shard strike.
Lore: Guardians stand unmoving for decades, watching over
ancient relics.

GRAVELIGHT WATCHER
------------------
Origin: Sentinels carved from relic stone.
Role: Ranged support.
Abilities: Light pulse, stone mark.
Lore: Watchers emit a pale glow that reveals hidden threats.

BOULDER-HEART TITAN
-------------------
Origin: Manifestation of Stone’s eternal memory.
Role: Colossal tank.
Abilities: Mountain crash, rune quake.
Lore: Titans move slowly, but each step reshapes the land.
