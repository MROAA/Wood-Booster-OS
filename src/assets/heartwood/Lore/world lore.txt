============================================================
                     HEARTHWOOD — WORLD LORE
============================================================

INTRODUCTION
------------
Hearthwood is an ancient forest-realm whose roots reach deeper
than any kingdom remembers. It is not merely a place, but a
living world shaped by four primal forces: Saplight, Hollow,
Ember, and Stone. These forces are older than civilization,
older than written history, older even than the gods that
wandered the early ages.

Hearthwood breathes, remembers, and reacts. Every root, every
shadow, every stone carries a fragment of its will.

------------------------------------------------------------
THE FOURFOLD WILDS — PRIMAL FORCES OF THE WORLD
------------------------------------------------------------

SAPLIGHT (WOOD)
---------------
Saplight is the force of growth, renewal, and living memory.
It flows through roots, moss, and leaves like a warm pulse.
Saplight druids claim it is the forest’s breath — the rhythm
that keeps Hearthwood alive.

Saplight is not gentle. It is relentless life, pushing through
stone, reclaiming ruins, and healing wounds that should not
heal.

HOLLOW (WRAITH)
---------------
Hollow is the force of absence — the echo left behind when
something is lost. It is cold, violet, and quiet. Hollow is
not evil; it is the memory of what once was, the shadow of
forgotten things.

Where Saplight grows, Hollow erodes. Where Hollow whispers,
Saplight listens.

EMBER (TOXIC)
-------------
Ember is the force of fire, ash, and transformation. It is the
remnant of ancient rituals that once reshaped the land. Ember
burns, but it also purifies. Toxic is Ember’s corrupted twin —
the rot that follows uncontrolled flame.

Ember tribes believe destruction is necessary for rebirth.
Toxic cults believe corruption is the truest form of change.

STONE (RELIC)
-------------
Stone is the force of memory, permanence, and the weight of
ages. It is the backbone of Hearthwood — the bones of the
world. Stone holds the oldest runes, the first relics, and the
truths no tribe dares to speak aloud.

Stone does not change. It endures.

------------------------------------------------------------
THE AGE OF ROOTS — EARLY HISTORY
------------------------------------------------------------
Before tribes, before kingdoms, Hearthwood was shaped by the
Fourfold Wilds. Saplight grew the first forests. Hollow carved
the first shadows. Ember scorched the first clearings. Stone
rose into monoliths that still stand today.

The earliest beings were not mortals. They were manifestations
of the forces themselves — Rootbound Titans, Hollow Wraiths,
Ember Spirits, and Stone Golems.

These beings vanished long before recorded history, leaving
behind relics, runes, and echoes.

------------------------------------------------------------
THE TRIBAL ERA — RISE OF THE FOUR HEIMOS
------------------------------------------------------------
As mortals emerged, they aligned themselves with the primal
forces. Four tribes formed:

Saplight Tribe — guardians of growth
Hollow Tribe — keepers of forgotten echoes
Ember Tribe — wielders of fire and change
Stone Tribe — protectors of ancient memory

Their alliances and conflicts shaped Hearthwood’s borders.

------------------------------------------------------------
GEOGRAPHY OF HEARTHWOOD
------------------------------------------------------------

THE GREEN VEINS
---------------
A vast network of living roots beneath the forest floor.
Saplight druids travel through these tunnels like rivers.

THE HOLLOW MARSH
----------------
A violet swamp where echoes linger. Wraiths drift between
trees, whispering memories of the dead.

THE EMBER SCAR
--------------
A burned wasteland that never cools. Toxic fumes rise from
cracks in the earth.

THE RELIC SPINE
---------------
A mountain range of monoliths carved with runes older than
language.

------------------------------------------------------------
THE GREAT FRACTURE — WORLD-SHAPING EVENT
------------------------------------------------------------
A cataclysm that split Hearthwood’s primal forces apart.
Saplight surged uncontrollably. Hollow expanded into the
marshlands. Ember ignited the Scar. Stone cracked into
thousands of relics.

The tribes still blame each other.

------------------------------------------------------------
CURRENT ERA — THE AGE OF AWAKENING
------------------------------------------------------------
Something stirs beneath Hearthwood.

Roots tremble. Shadows thicken. Embers glow. Stones hum.

The primal forces are awakening again — and the tribes must
choose whether to unite or repeat the mistakes of the past.
