ECHO HERALD — STORY INTRO

The forest does not greet you with words.

It greets you with memory.

A pulse travels through the roots beneath your feet — a soft,
blue shimmer that rises through the moss like breath. The air
vibrates. The trees lean in. The shadows shift, not away from
you, but toward you, as if recognizing something they lost.

You take a step.

The pulse follows.

Another step.

The shadows answer.

The forest has been waiting.

A whisper moves through the canopy, not carried by wind but by
intention. It is not a voice. It is not language. It is the
feeling of something ancient remembering your name before you
ever spoke it.

Marc.

The forest remembers you incorrectly.

A second pulse rises — stronger, colder. The Hollow Marsh
stirs. Echoes drift between the trees, fragments of moments
that have not yet happened. They circle you, curious, cautious,
as if trying to decide whether you are past or future.

A third pulse ignites — hot, sharp. Ember Scar breathes out a
thin veil of ash that rises instead of falling. The ash forms
shapes. Faces. Memories. Warnings.

A fourth pulse hums — deep, resonant. Stone monoliths awaken
in the distance, their runes rearranging themselves as if
rewriting the world’s history.

Four pulses.

Four forces.

And then — a fifth.

It does not rise from the ground.
It does not fall from the sky.
It simply *appears*, folding reality inward like a page being
turned.

Verge.

The boundary between what is real and what is remembered.

The pulse touches you.

And the forest speaks.

“Herald… the memory is happening out of order.”

Your shadow moves before you do.
Your reflection steps forward without waiting.
Your echo answers a question you have not yet asked.

The forest continues.

“Herald… your footsteps exist in two timelines.”

The pulse intensifies.
The world bends.
The trees blur.
The shadows split.
The ash freezes.
The monoliths crack.
The Verge opens.

And you fall into it.

Not downward.
Not upward.
But sideways — into a place where time is soft and memory is
loud.

You land on a surface that is not ground.
A surface made of moments.
A surface made of choices.
A surface made of echoes.

Your shadow stands beside you now — not behind you.
It watches.
It waits.
It remembers.

The forest speaks one last time.

“Herald… the world is rewriting itself.
You must decide which memories remain.”

You breathe in.

The pulse answers.

Your story begins.
