============================================================
                     HEARTHWOOD — RELICS LORE
============================================================

INTRODUCTION
------------
Relics are the oldest artifacts in Hearthwood. They were not
crafted by mortals, nor shaped by tribes. They are remnants of
the primal forces themselves — fragments of Saplight, Hollow,
Ember, and Stone that solidified into physical form during the
Age of Roots.

Relics do not merely grant power. They remember.

============================================================
SAPLIGHT RELICS — BREATH OF THE FOREST
============================================================

GROVEHEART RELIC
----------------
Origin: Formed when the first forest took breath.
Power: Massive healing, root awakening, forest-wide renewal.
Lore: The Groveheart pulses like a living heart. When placed
in soil, entire groves rise in minutes. Saplight elders claim
it contains the memory of the first tree.

BRAMBLEHIDE STANDARD
--------------------
Origin: Woven from thorned vines during the first tribal war.
Power: Rallying aura, thorn armor, growth surge.
Lore: The Standard is carried only by Saplight champions. Its
presence causes bramble to grow around allies.

MOSS-ETCHED IDOL
----------------
Origin: A statue carved by the forest itself.
Power: Regeneration field, moss shield.
Lore: The Idol grows moss even in deserts. It is said to weep
when the forest is wounded.

ROOTBOUND TOTEM
----------------
Origin: A totem grown from living roots.
Power: Root eruption, entangling field.
Lore: Rootbound Totems are planted at sacred sites. They bind
intruders with living roots.

============================================================
HOLLOW RELICS — ECHO OF WHAT WAS LOST
============================================================

SHADE-ETCHED RELIC
------------------
Origin: Carved by shadows during the first eclipse.
Power: Echo strike, spectral veil, fear pulse.
Lore: The Relic whispers constantly. Hollow ritualists claim
it speaks in the voices of forgotten souls.

WRAITH-BOUND EFFIGY
-------------------
Origin: A statue inhabited by a trapped spirit.
Power: Wraith summon, spectral shield.
Lore: The Effigy’s eyes glow when danger approaches. Some say
it moves when unobserved.

HOLLOW-SIGIL SHARD
------------------
Origin: A fragment of the first Hollow monolith.
Power: Shadow burst, echo duplication.
Lore: The Shard is cold even in flame. It amplifies Hollow
magic beyond safe limits.

ECHO-BONE TOTEM
---------------
Origin: A totem carved from the bones of ancient beasts.
Power: Spirit call, echo resonance.
Lore: Echo-Bone Totems vibrate when spirits pass nearby.

============================================================
EMBER RELICS — ASH THAT REMAKES THE WORLD
============================================================

BLIGHT-ETCHED IDOL
------------------
Origin: Formed when Ember and Toxic forces collided.
Power: Corruption wave, toxic burst, flame rot.
Lore: The Idol spreads corruption wherever it is placed. Saplight
druids consider it the forest’s greatest wound.

EMBER-HEART SIGIL
-----------------
Origin: A burning sigil forged in ritual flame.
Power: Flame surge, ash shield.
Lore: The Sigil never cools. It glows brighter when Ember magic
is nearby.

CINDER-ROOT TOTEM
-----------------
Origin: A totem grown from fire-touched roots.
Power: Flame eruption, burning aura.
Lore: Cinder-Root Totems are used in Ember initiation rites.

ASH-RUNE TABLET
---------------
Origin: A stone tablet scorched by ancient firestorms.
Power: Rune ignition, ash burst.
Lore: The Tablet’s runes ignite when read aloud.

============================================================
STONE RELICS — MEMORY OF THE WORLD
============================================================

RUNBOUND MONOLITH
-----------------
Origin: One of the first monoliths raised by Stone.
Power: Rune amplification, stability field, earth pulse.
Lore: The Monolith contains runes older than language. Stone
elders claim it remembers the world’s creation.

EARTHSHARD RELIQUARY
--------------------
Origin: A container carved from relic stone.
Power: Memory storage, rune enhancement.
Lore: Some Reliquaries contain memories of events no tribe
wishes to recall.

GRAVELIGHT SIGIL
----------------
Origin: A glowing stone carved from the Relic Spine.
Power: Light pulse, hidden threat detection.
Lore: Gravelight Sigils reveal illusions and Hollow echoes.

BOULDER-HEART IDOL
------------------
Origin: A statue carved from the heart of a mountain.
Power: Tremor field, stone armor.
Lore: Boulder-Heart Idols hum softly, resonating with Stone
Titans.
