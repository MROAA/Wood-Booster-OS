HEARTHWOOD LORE CODEX — FULL BOOK (v1)

I. INTRODUCTION — THE LIVING REALM
Hearthwood is not a kingdom, nor a map, nor a place. It is a living ecosystem that remembers every root, every shadow, every stone, and every ember. It is the oldest entity in the world — not a god, not a spirit, but the collective consciousness of nature itself. When the four primal forces were born, Hearthwood awakened.

II. THE FOURFOLD WILDS — PRIMAL FORCES

SAPLIGHT — The Breath of Life
Growth, light, renewal, roots. Saplight is relentless life-force pushing through stone and healing what the world loses.

HOLLOW — The Echo of Absence
Shadow, memory, loss, echo. Hollow is not evil; it is the imprint of what once existed.

EMBER — The Ash of Change
Fire, ash, ritual, transformation. Ember burns to create. Toxic is its corrupted twin — rot, corruption, poison.

STONE — The Memory of Ages
Rock, permanence, runes, relics. Stone does not change; it endures.

III. THE AGE OF ROOTS — WORLD ORIGIN
Before tribes, before mortals, before history:
Saplight grew the first forests.
Hollow carved the first shadows.
Ember scorched the first clearings.
Stone raised the first monoliths.

The earliest beings were primal entities:
Rootbound Titans, Hollow Wraiths, Ember Spirits, Stone Golems.
They vanished, leaving relics and echoes.

IV. THE TRIBAL ERA — RISE OF THE FOUR TRIBES

SAPLIGHT TRIBE — Breath of the Forest
Healers, druids, guardians of growth.

HOLLOW TRIBE — Echo of What Was Lost
Keepers of forgotten memories and spectral forces.

EMBER TRIBE — Ash That Remakes the World
Ritual fire, transformation, corruption.

STONE TRIBE — Memory of the World
Protectors of monoliths, runes, and ancient truths.

V. THE GREAT FRACTURE — WORLD-SHAPING EVENT
A cataclysm split the primal forces apart:
Saplight surged uncontrollably.
Hollow expanded into marshlands.
Ember ignited the Scar.
Stone shattered into relics.

The tribes blame each other. The truth is unknown.

VI. THE AGE OF AWAKENING — CURRENT ERA
Something stirs beneath Hearthwood.
Roots tremble. Shadows thicken. Embers glow. Stones hum.
The primal forces are awakening again.

VII. TRIBE CODEX — FULL TRIBAL LORE

HOLLOW TRIBE
