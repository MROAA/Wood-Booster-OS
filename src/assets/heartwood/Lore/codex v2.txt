============================================================
                     HEARTHWOOD — RUNES LORE
============================================================

INTRODUCTION
------------
Runes are the oldest language in Hearthwood. They were not
invented by tribes or carved by mortals. They emerged when the
Fourfold Wilds first collided — symbols formed from pure
primal force.

Each rune carries memory, power, and intent. To carve a rune
is to speak directly to the world.

============================================================
SAPLIGHT RUNES — BREATH OF THE FOREST
============================================================

SAPLIGHT GLYPH
--------------
Origin: Formed when the first pulse of life spread through the
roots of Hearthwood.
Meaning: Growth, renewal, healing.
Power: Restores vitality, strengthens allies, awakens roots.
Lore: Saplight Glyphs glow softly when placed near living wood.
Druids claim they “breathe” in rhythm with the forest.

ROOTWEAVE RUNE
--------------
Origin: Woven into the first living roots.
Meaning: Connection, unity, living bonds.
Power: Binds allies together, entangles foes.
Lore: Rootweave Runes are used in rituals of protection. They
tighten when danger approaches.

GROVE-MARK TABLET
-----------------
Origin: A rune carved by the forest itself.
Meaning: Territory, sanctuary.
Power: Creates zones of protection and rapid growth.
Lore: Grove-Marks are placed at sacred sites to mark the
forest’s domain.

============================================================
HOLLOW RUNES — ECHO OF WHAT WAS LOST
============================================================

SHADECOIL RUNE
--------------
Origin: Formed from the first shadow that ever fell.
Meaning: Echo-binding, spectral tether.
Power: Binds spirits, amplifies echoes, weakens light.
Lore: Shadecoil Runes hum quietly. Hollow ritualists say they
contain “the memory of absence.”

HOLLOW-SCRIPT SIGIL
-------------------
Origin: Written by the Hollow Marsh itself during the first
eclipse.
Meaning: Void, silence, forgetting.
Power: Creates zones of silence, erases magical traces.
Lore: Hollow-Script Sigils are feared by Saplight druids, who
claim they “teach the forest to forget.”

ECHO-MARK TABLET
----------------
Origin: A tablet carved from shadow-infused stone.
Meaning: Memory, resonance.
Power: Amplifies spectral forces, reveals hidden echoes.
Lore: Echo-Marks glow when spirits pass nearby.

============================================================
EMBER RUNES — ASH THAT REMAKES THE WORLD
============================================================

EMBERFLARE RUNE
---------------
Origin: Born from the first ritual flame.
Meaning: Fire, ignition, transformation.
Power: Ignites flame, enhances Ember magic, burns corruption.
Lore: Emberflare Runes heat the air around them. They pulse
when Ember Spirits draw near.

BLIGHT-SCRIPT SIGIL
-------------------
Origin: Formed when Ember and Toxic forces collided.
Meaning: Corruption, decay, mutation.
Power: Spreads blight, weakens healing, mutates flora.
Lore: Blight-Script Sigils are forbidden in Saplight territory.

ASH-SCRIPT GLYPH
----------------
Origin: Etched into stone during ancient firestorms.
Meaning: Ash, memory of flame.
Power: Creates ash clouds, shields allies with ember dust.
Lore: Ash-Script Glyphs ignite when spoken aloud.

============================================================
STONE RUNES — MEMORY OF THE WORLD
============================================================

EARTHBIND GLYPH
---------------
Origin: Carved into the first monolith.
Meaning: Stability, grounding, permanence.
Power: Stabilizes terrain, strengthens defenses, halts movement.
Lore: Earthbind Glyphs resonate with Stone Titans.

STONE-FRACT RUNE
----------------
Origin: Formed when the Relic Spine cracked during the Great
Fracture.
Meaning: Endurance, fracture, ancient memory.
Power: Creates shockwaves, reinforces stone, reveals hidden
runes.
Lore: Stone-Fract Runes are used to awaken dormant relics.

RUNEBREAK TABLET
----------------
Origin: A shattered tablet from the Age of Roots.
Meaning: Disruption, raw primal force.
Power: Breaks magical bindings, destabilizes runic fields.
Lore: Stone elders forbid its use, claiming it “remembers too
much.”
