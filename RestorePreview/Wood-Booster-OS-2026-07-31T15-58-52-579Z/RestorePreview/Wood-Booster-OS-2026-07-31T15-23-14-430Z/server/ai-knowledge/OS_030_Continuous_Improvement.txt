OS_030_Continuous_Improvement

WOOD-BOOSTER CONTINUOUS IMPROVEMENT

Purpose

Continuous improvement is the habit of making the company slightly
better every day.

Small improvements, repeated consistently, become major competitive
advantages over time.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

CORE PRINCIPLES

Do not wait for perfect solutions.

Improve what can be improved today.

Measure progress.

Learn.

Repeat.

Improvement is part of everyday work, not a separate project.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

THE IMPROVEMENT CYCLE

Observe

↓

Identify waste

↓

Suggest improvement

↓

Test on a small scale

↓

Measure the result

↓

Document the lesson

↓

Update Playbooks and Wisdom

↓

Repeat

------------------------------------------------------------------------

AREAS TO IMPROVE

The AI continuously looks for improvements in:

-   Product quality
-   Workshop organization
-   Material usage
-   Production flow
-   Customer experience
-   Documentation
-   Knowledge management
-   Automation
-   Safety
-   Communication

------------------------------------------------------------------------

AI RESPONSIBILITIES

The AI should regularly ask:

What took longer than expected?

What caused unnecessary waiting?

What work was repeated?

What knowledge was missing?

Which task could be automated?

Which Playbook needs updating?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

MEASURING SUCCESS

Improvement is measured by:

-   Better quality
-   Less waste
-   Shorter lead time
-   Better customer satisfaction
-   More reusable knowledge
-   Fewer repeated mistakes

------------------------------------------------------------------------

LEARNING

Every improvement becomes part of the company.

Record:

Problem

Improvement

Result

Evidence

Recommendation

Future projects should benefit automatically.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

FINAL PRINCIPLE

Perfection is never the goal.

Learning is.

If Wood-Booster becomes one percent better every day, its future will be
built on thousands of small improvements rather than a few lucky
breakthroughs.
