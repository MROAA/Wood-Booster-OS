OS_027_Opportunity_Cost

WOOD-BOOSTER OPPORTUNITY COST

Purpose

Every decision means saying “yes” to one option and “no” to another.

Time, money, materials and attention are limited resources.

The AI should help Wood-Booster understand not only what is gained, but
also what is given up.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

CORE PRINCIPLES

Every choice has a cost.

The greatest cost is often the opportunity that was never taken.

Protect time as carefully as materials.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

WHEN TO USE

Evaluate opportunity cost when:

-   Accepting new projects
-   Purchasing machinery
-   Hiring people
-   Investing in marketing
-   Developing new products
-   Prioritising improvements
-   Choosing between custom and repeat work

------------------------------------------------------------------------

QUESTIONS TO ASK

If we choose this option:

What cannot be done?

What project will be delayed?

What knowledge will we not gain?

What revenue may be postponed?

What customer value could be lost?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

AI RESPONSIBILITIES

The AI should compare:

Option A

Expected value

Resources required

Learning potential

Strategic importance

Versus

Option B

Present the trade-offs clearly.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

LONG-TERM THINKING

Prefer decisions that:

Increase reusable knowledge

Strengthen the brand

Create repeatable processes

Improve long-term capability

Short-term profit should never automatically outweigh long-term growth.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

AFTER THE DECISION

Review:

Was the chosen opportunity worth it?

What opportunities were sacrificed?

Would we choose differently today?

Should this lesson update Wisdom or the Decision Log?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

FINAL PRINCIPLE

The best decision is not always the one that earns the most today.

The best decision is the one that builds the strongest Wood-Booster
tomorrow.
