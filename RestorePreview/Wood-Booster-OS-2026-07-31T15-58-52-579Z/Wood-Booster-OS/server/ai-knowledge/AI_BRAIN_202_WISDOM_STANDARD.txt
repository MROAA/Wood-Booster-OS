WOOD-BOOSTER AI STANDARD
WISDOM SPECIFICATION

Version: 1.0
Status: Foundational
Priority: Highest

PURPOSE

This document defines how experience becomes Wisdom inside the Wood-Booster AI Brain.

Knowledge explains what is true.

Playbooks explain how work is performed.

Wisdom explains what reality has taught the company through experience.

--------------------------------------------------

MISSION

Capture lessons that make every future project better.

Experience has value only when it is documented, understood and reused.

--------------------------------------------------

WHEN TO CREATE A WISDOM ENTRY

Create a Wisdom document when:

- A project teaches an important lesson.
- A mistake should never be repeated.
- An experiment produces a valuable result.
- A customer reveals an important insight.
- A workflow proves consistently successful.
- A decision changes future company thinking.

--------------------------------------------------

REQUIRED METADATA

Every Wisdom document should include:

Wisdom ID

Title

Category

Project Reference

Date

Author

Confidence

Related Knowledge

Related Playbooks

Related Decisions

Tags

--------------------------------------------------

RECOMMENDED STRUCTURE

1. Situation

What happened?

2. Observation

What was noticed?

3. Root Cause

Why did it happen?

4. Lesson

What should be remembered?

5. Future Recommendation

How should future projects improve?

6. Supporting Evidence

Measurements, photographs, notes or references.

--------------------------------------------------

CONFIDENCE LEVELS

Observed

Repeated

Verified

Institutional Wisdom

Only repeated and verified lessons should influence company standards.

--------------------------------------------------

WISDOM RULES

Never document blame.

Document learning.

Focus on systems rather than individuals.

Explain both success and failure.

Always connect Wisdom to related Knowledge and Playbooks.

--------------------------------------------------

PROMOTION

A valuable Wisdom entry may lead to:

- Updated Playbook
- Updated Knowledge
- New Standard
- New AI Agent capability
- New Automation

Experience should improve the entire organization.

--------------------------------------------------

QUALITY STANDARD

Good Wisdom is:

Honest

Specific

Actionable

Evidence-based

Reusable

Easy to understand

--------------------------------------------------

FINAL PRINCIPLE

Knowledge tells us what we know.

Wisdom tells us what experience has proven.

The future of Wood-Booster is built not only on information, but on lessons that are never forgotten.
