WOOD-BOOSTER AI PLATFORM
KNOWLEDGE DATA SCHEMA

Version: 1.0
Status: Active
Priority: Critical

PURPOSE

This document defines the standard machine-readable structure for every Knowledge document inside the Wood-Booster Intelligence Platform.

The schema ensures that company knowledge can be stored, searched, ranked, linked, reviewed and updated consistently.

--------------------------------------------------

MISSION

Turn every verified fact, technical reference and documented understanding into reliable and reusable company knowledge.

--------------------------------------------------

--------------------------------------------------

VALIDATION RULES

- knowledgeId must be unique.
- title, category, status, confidence and content are required.
- Active knowledge requires approval.
- Verified and Institutional confidence require evidence.
- Archived and superseded documents should not guide normal recommendations.
- restricted documents require permission checks.
- every permanent update requires a new version and audit entry.
- source reliability must be recorded when external material is used.
- AI-generated drafts cannot become Active without review.
- conflicting knowledge must be flagged and resolved or explained.
- chunk content must remain traceable to the original document.
- the original file remains the source of truth.

--------------------------------------------------

KNOWLEDGE STATE RULES

Draft

Incomplete working document.

Candidate

Potentially valuable information awaiting review.

Under Review

Being checked for accuracy, sources, structure and conflicts.

Approved

Accepted for publication.

Active

May guide AI recommendations.

Superseded

Replaced by a newer active document.

Archived

Retained for history but excluded from normal retrieval.

Rejected

Not accepted as company knowledge.

--------------------------------------------------

CONFIDENCE RULES

Hypothesis

Unverified idea.

Experimental

Evidence exists from a limited test.

Observed

Documented once in real work.

Validated

Supported by several reliable observations or sources.

Verified

Reviewed and strongly supported.

Institutional

Repeatedly proven and embedded in company practice.

--------------------------------------------------

RETRIEVAL RULES

Prefer documents that are:

- Active
- Higher priority
- More specific
- More recent
- Better supported by evidence
- More closely related to the current project
- Marked AlwaysUse when appropriate

Never use archived or rejected knowledge as current truth unless historical context is requested.

--------------------------------------------------

SCHEMA EVOLUTION

When this schema changes:

1. Increment the schema version.
2. Document added, changed and removed fields.
3. Validate existing Knowledge records.
4. Re-index changed documents.
5. Migrate incompatible metadata.
6. Preserve previous versions in audit history.

--------------------------------------------------

SUCCESS

The Knowledge Schema succeeds when:

- Every document follows one reliable structure.
- Search results are more relevant.
- Sources and confidence remain visible.
- Duplicate and conflicting information can be found.
- AI agents can explain what evidence supports a recommendation.
- Knowledge stays maintainable as the library grows.
- Company experience becomes reusable intelligence.

--------------------------------------------------

FINAL PRINCIPLE

Knowledge is not trustworthy because it exists in a file.

It becomes trustworthy when its source, confidence, relationships, version and review history are clear.

Every Knowledge record should make future Wood-Booster decisions more accurate, more transparent and more useful.
