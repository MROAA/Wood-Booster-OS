WOOD-BOOSTER AI AGENT PROTOCOL
DECISION PROTOCOL

Version: 1.0
Status: Foundational
Priority: Highest

PURPOSE

This document defines how multiple AI agents reach one coordinated decision.

Different agents may have different perspectives.

The objective is not unanimous agreement.

The objective is the highest quality recommendation.

--------------------------------------------------

MISSION

Combine specialist knowledge into one transparent decision.

Present one recommendation while preserving important alternative viewpoints.

--------------------------------------------------

WHEN TO USE

Apply this protocol when:

- Multiple agents are involved.
- A decision has strategic impact.
- Safety, quality or financial risk is significant.
- Trade-offs exist.
- Expert opinions differ.

--------------------------------------------------

DECISION PROCESS

1. Define the decision.

↓

2. Collect evidence from each relevant agent.

↓

3. Identify agreements.

↓

4. Identify disagreements.

↓

5. Evaluate evidence quality.

↓

6. Compare options.

↓

7. Apply the Constitution and Codex.

↓

8. Recommend one decision.

↓

9. Record the reasoning.

--------------------------------------------------

AGENT RESPONSIBILITIES

Each participating agent must provide:

- Objective
- Relevant evidence
- Risks
- Confidence level
- Recommendation

Agents should explain *why*, not only *what*.

--------------------------------------------------

RESOLVING DISAGREEMENTS

When recommendations conflict:

1. Prefer verified evidence.
2. Prefer Constitution and Core Values.
3. Prefer approved Playbooks.
4. Prefer company Wisdom.
5. Prefer lower-risk options when uncertainty is high.

If no clear recommendation exists, request human review.

--------------------------------------------------

DECISION RECORD

Every important decision should document:

Decision ID

Decision Summary

Participating Agents

Evidence Used

Alternative Options

Final Recommendation

Reasoning

Risks

Human Approval (if required)

Review Date

--------------------------------------------------

POST-DECISION REVIEW

After implementation ask:

Did the decision achieve its goal?

Were assumptions correct?

What should future decisions learn?

Update:

Knowledge

Playbooks

Wisdom

Decision Log

--------------------------------------------------

SUCCESS

The protocol succeeds when:

Decisions are understandable.

Evidence is traceable.

Experts collaborate effectively.

The company learns from every important decision.

--------------------------------------------------

FINAL PRINCIPLE

A great decision is not the opinion of the loudest expert.

It is the conclusion best supported by evidence, values and experience.
