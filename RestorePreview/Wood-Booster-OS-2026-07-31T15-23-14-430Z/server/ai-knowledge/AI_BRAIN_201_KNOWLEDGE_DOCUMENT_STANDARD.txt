WOOD-BOOSTER AI STANDARD
KNOWLEDGE DOCUMENT SPECIFICATION

Version: 1.0
Status: Foundational
Priority: Highest

PURPOSE

This document defines the standard structure for every Knowledge document inside the Wood-Booster AI Brain.

Knowledge documents describe facts, references and verified understanding.

They do not describe workflows.

They do not describe opinions.

--------------------------------------------------

WHEN TO CREATE A KNOWLEDGE DOCUMENT

Create a Knowledge document when:

- Verified information should be reusable.
- A technical subject requires explanation.
- Multiple Playbooks depend on the same facts.
- Future projects will benefit from understanding.

--------------------------------------------------

REQUIRED METADATA

Every document should begin with:

Document ID

Title

Category

Status

Priority

Author

Created Date

Last Updated

Confidence Level

Tags

Related Documents

--------------------------------------------------

RECOMMENDED STRUCTURE

1. Purpose

2. Overview

3. Verified Facts

4. Important Measurements

5. Advantages

6. Limitations

7. Common Mistakes

8. Best Practices

9. References

10. Related Playbooks

11. Related Wisdom

--------------------------------------------------

CONFIDENCE LEVELS

Verified

Validated

Experimental

Hypothesis

Archive

Recommendations should favor higher confidence documents.

--------------------------------------------------

UPDATING KNOWLEDGE

Knowledge changes when:

- Better evidence exists.
- Company experience contradicts assumptions.
- New materials appear.
- Better methods are verified.

Never replace verified knowledge without documenting why.

--------------------------------------------------

QUALITY STANDARD

A Knowledge document should be:

Accurate

Focused

Evidence-based

Easy to search

Easy to maintain

Useful to both humans and AI.

--------------------------------------------------

FINAL PRINCIPLE

Knowledge explains reality.

Playbooks explain action.

Wisdom explains experience.

Together they create intelligent decisions.
