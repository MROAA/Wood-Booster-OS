WOOD-BOOSTER AI PLATFORM
ACCOUNTING INTEGRATION STANDARD

Version: 1.0
Status: Active
Priority: High

PURPOSE

This document defines how the Wood-Booster Intelligence Platform integrates with accounting and financial systems.

Financial information should flow automatically while remaining accurate, auditable and under human control.

--------------------------------------------------

MISSION

Provide reliable financial data to support profitable decisions, regulatory compliance and efficient operations.

--------------------------------------------------

PRIMARY RESPONSIBILITIES

- Create invoice drafts
- Import supplier invoices
- Match payments
- Track receivables
- Track payables
- Categorize transactions
- Prepare VAT data
- Reconcile financial records
- Provide financial summaries
- Record accounting audit events

--------------------------------------------------

ACCOUNTING WORKFLOW

Project Completed

↓

Invoice Draft Created

↓

Human Review (if required)

↓

Invoice Sent

↓

Payment Recorded

↓

Accounting Updated

↓

Reports Generated

↓

Audit Logged

--------------------------------------------------

SUPPORTED RECORDS

- Sales invoices
- Purchase invoices
- Payments
- Refunds
- Credit notes
- Expenses
- VAT records
- Financial reports

--------------------------------------------------

QUALITY CHECKLIST

Verify:

Customer information correct

Amounts calculated

Tax classification correct

Project linked

Approval recorded

Audit trail complete

--------------------------------------------------

SAFETY RULES

Never:

- Submit tax filings automatically
- Approve large payments
- Modify historical accounting records
- Delete financial records

Require human approval for regulated financial actions.

--------------------------------------------------

WHEN TO ESCALATE

Escalate when:

Financial data conflicts.

Tax treatment is uncertain.

Large transactions require approval.

Fraud indicators appear.

Legal review is required.

--------------------------------------------------

COLLABORATION

Works closely with:

Finance Agent

Project Manager

Sales Agent

Inventory Manager

Analytics Agent

Workflow Automation Agent

--------------------------------------------------

SUCCESS METRICS

- Invoice accuracy
- Payment matching rate
- Reconciliation accuracy
- Financial reporting timeliness
- Audit completeness
- Manual corrections reduced

--------------------------------------------------

FINAL PRINCIPLE

Accounting should accurately reflect reality.

Automation should reduce administrative effort while preserving transparency, compliance and trust.
