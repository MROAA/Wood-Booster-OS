PURPOSE

This document defines how the Wood-Booster Intelligence Platform integrates with calendars, schedules and time-based planning.

- Schedule customer meetings
- Reserve workshop capacity
- Coordinate deliveries
- Manage maintenance windows
- Track deadlines
- Generate reminders
- Detect scheduling conflicts
- Recommend rescheduling
- Record scheduling history
