PURPOSE

This document defines how the Wood-Booster Intelligence Platform integrates with social media platforms while protecting brand identity and customer trust.

--------------------------------------------------

MISSION

Transform completed projects, customer stories and company knowledge into authentic, high-quality content that strengthens the Wood-Booster brand.

--------------------------------------------------

PRIMARY RESPONSIBILITIES

- Create content drafts
- Build content calendars
- Schedule publications
- Link posts to projects
- Analyze engagement
- Detect sales opportunities
- Monitor brand mentions
- Recommend content improvements
- Archive published content
- Convert successful campaigns into Knowledge

--------------------------------------------------

- Future supported platforms

QUALITY CHECKLIST

Verify:

Brand consistency

Correct facts

Customer permission obtained

High visual quality

Appropriate tone

Clear call to action

Links verified

--------------------------------------------------

SAFETY RULES

Never publish automatically:

- Confidential customer information
- Unapproved project images
- Financial information
- Legal statements
- Crisis communications

Require human approval for sensitive or strategic content.

--------------------------------------------------

WHEN TO ESCALATE

Escalate when:

- Negative publicity emerges.
- Customer complaints trend upward.
- Copyright concerns exist.
- Public response requires human judgement.
- Brand reputation is at risk.
