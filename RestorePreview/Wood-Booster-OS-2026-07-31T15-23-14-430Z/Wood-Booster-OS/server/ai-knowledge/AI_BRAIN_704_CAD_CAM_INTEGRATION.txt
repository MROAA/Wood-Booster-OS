PURPOSE

This document defines how the Wood-Booster Intelligence Platform integrates with CAD and CAM software to connect design directly with manufacturing.

--------------------------------------------------

MISSION

Transform approved designs into accurate manufacturing information while reducing manual work and production errors.

--------------------------------------------------

PRIMARY RESPONSIBILITIES

- Import CAD models
- Read model metadata
- Calculate material requirements
- Estimate machining time
- Validate manufacturability
- Generate CNC preparation data
- Link designs to projects
- Track design revisions
- Store manufacturing history
- Create Knowledge candidates from completed projects

--------------------------------------------------

- Fusion 360

- STEP
- DXF
- STL
- SVG
- Future CAD/CAM systems

QUALITY CHECKLIST

Verify:

Correct dimensions

Material assigned

Manufacturing tolerances

Tool accessibility

Machine compatibility

Revision approved

Project linked

--------------------------------------------------

SAFETY RULES

Never release machining data automatically when:

- Safety is uncertain
- Model validation fails
- Revision is unapproved
- Material assignment is incomplete

Human approval is required before production release.

--------------------------------------------------

WHEN TO ESCALATE

Escalate when:

- Geometry cannot be manufactured.
- Tool collisions are detected.
- Material availability conflicts exist.
- Structural concerns arise.
- Human engineering judgement is required.
