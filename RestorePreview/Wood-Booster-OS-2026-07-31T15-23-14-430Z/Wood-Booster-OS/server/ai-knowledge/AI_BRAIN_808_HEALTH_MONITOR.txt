PURPOSE

This document defines how the Wood-Booster Intelligence Platform continuously monitors its own health, reliability and operational readiness.

Health monitoring allows the platform to detect problems early, recover safely and provide confidence to users and administrators.

--------------------------------------------------

MISSION

Continuously observe the entire platform and maintain reliable operation through monitoring, alerting and self-healing.

SELF-HEALING ACTIONS

When safe, the platform may:

- Restart unhealthy services
- Retry failed connections
- Switch to fallback AI models
- Clear expired caches
- Rebalance workloads
- Isolate failed plugins
- Reassign blocked tasks

Human approval is required for destructive recovery actions.

--------------------------------------------------

ESCALATION RULES

Escalate when:

- Automatic recovery fails.
- Critical services become unavailable.
- Data integrity is threatened.
- Security incidents are detected.
- Platform reliability falls below target.
