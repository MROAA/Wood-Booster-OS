AI_BRAIN_903_CUSTOMER_SCHEMA

WOOD-BOOSTER AI PLATFORM
CUSTOMER DATA SCHEMA

Version: 1.0
Status: Active
Priority: Critical

PURPOSE

This document defines the standard machine-readable structure for every customer record inside the Wood-Booster Intelligence Platform.

The schema allows customer information, communication history, projects, preferences, consent, support cases and long-term relationship data to be managed consistently.

--------------------------------------------------

MISSION

Maintain one trusted customer record that helps Wood-Booster understand, serve and communicate with every customer responsibly.

--------------------------------------------------

--------------------------------------------------

VALIDATION RULES

- customerId must be unique.
- name is required.
- primary contact must include at least one valid contact method.
- status must use an allowed value.
- business customers should include company name.
- marketing messages require valid consent.
- restricted customer data must follow the Security Runtime and Permission Protocol.
- archived customers are read-only unless restored.
- deletion requests require human review and an audit record.
- financial summaries must be synchronized from approved financial data.
- unsupported personal profiling is prohibited.

--------------------------------------------------

PRIVACY RULES

Store only information that supports a legitimate business purpose.

Do not store sensitive personal information unless necessary, lawful and approved.

Do not infer protected personal characteristics.

Respect consent, retention and deletion requirements.

Customer trust is more important than collecting excessive data.

--------------------------------------------------

CUSTOMER STATE RULES

Lead

Initial contact exists, but qualification is incomplete.

Qualified

Need, budget, timing or suitability has been confirmed.

Active

An active project, order or ongoing relationship exists.

Inactive

No current work, but the relationship remains valid.

At Risk

A documented issue threatens satisfaction or retention.

VIP

Strategically important relationship requiring approved enhanced service.

Former

Relationship has ended, but historical records remain relevant.

Archived

Record is preserved for legal or historical reasons and is normally read-only.

--------------------------------------------------

SCHEMA EVOLUTION

When this schema changes:

1. Increment the schema version.
2. Document added, changed and removed fields.
3. Validate existing customer records.
4. Migrate incompatible records.
5. Recheck privacy and consent requirements.
6. Preserve previous versions in the audit history.

--------------------------------------------------

SUCCESS

The Customer Schema succeeds when:

- Every customer has one reliable record.
- Communication history is easy to understand.
- Projects and quotations remain connected.
- Customer preferences improve service.
- Privacy and consent remain visible.
- AI agents can recommend relevant next actions.
- Customer relationships become stronger over time.

--------------------------------------------------

FINAL PRINCIPLE

A customer record is not a collection of data points.

It represents a real person or organization that has trusted Wood-Booster.

Every field should help the company serve that relationship with greater care, clarity and respect.
