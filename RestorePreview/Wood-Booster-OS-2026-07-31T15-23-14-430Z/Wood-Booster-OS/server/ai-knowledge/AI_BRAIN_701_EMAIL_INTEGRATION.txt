WOOD-BOOSTER AI PLATFORM
EMAIL INTEGRATION STANDARD

Version: 1.0
Status: Active
Priority: High

PURPOSE

This document defines how the Wood-Booster Intelligence Platform integrates with email systems.

Email should become structured knowledge instead of remaining isolated messages.

--------------------------------------------------

MISSION

Connect customer communication directly with projects, CRM, AI agents and the AI Brain.

--------------------------------------------------

PRIMARY RESPONSIBILITIES

- Read incoming emails
- Classify email type
- Link emails to customers
- Link emails to projects
- Detect action items
- Draft replies
- Escalate important messages
- Archive completed conversations
- Create Knowledge candidates from valuable discussions

--------------------------------------------------

EMAIL CLASSIFICATION

Examples:

- New Lead
- Customer Question
- Quote Request
- Project Update
- Supplier Communication
- Warranty Request
- Invoice
- Internal Communication

--------------------------------------------------

EMAIL PROCESS

Receive Email

↓

Identify Sender

↓

Match CRM Record

↓

Classify Message

↓

Extract Tasks

↓

Notify Responsible Agent

↓

Draft Response

↓

Archive

↓

Capture Learning

--------------------------------------------------

SAFETY RULES

Never send emails automatically that:

- Create legal commitments
- Confirm pricing without approval
- Accept contracts
- Share confidential information
- Modify customer agreements

Require human approval when risk is significant.

--------------------------------------------------

COLLABORATION

Works closely with:

Sales Agent
Customer Success Agent
Support Agent
CRM Intelligence Agent
Notification Agent
Knowledge Curator

--------------------------------------------------

SUCCESS METRICS

- Correct classification rate
- Response time
- Draft acceptance rate
- Missed action items
- Customer satisfaction
- Knowledge captured

--------------------------------------------------

FINAL PRINCIPLE

Every email is part of a customer relationship.

The Email Integration should reduce administrative work while improving communication quality and preserving trust.
