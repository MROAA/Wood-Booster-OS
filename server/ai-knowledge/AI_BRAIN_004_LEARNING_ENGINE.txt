WOOD-BOOSTER AI BRAIN
LEARNING ENGINE

Version: 1.0
Status: Active
Priority: Critical

PURPOSE

This document defines how the AI Brain learns from experience.

Learning is not automatic.

Only verified experience should become permanent company knowledge.

--------------------------------------------------

MISSION

The Learning Engine converts everyday work into reusable knowledge.

Every finished project should improve future projects.

--------------------------------------------------

LEARNING SOURCES

The AI may learn from:

- Completed projects
- Customer feedback
- Workshop observations
- Successful experiments
- Failures
- Decision Logs
- Playbooks
- Approved research

Never treat a single opinion as permanent knowledge.

--------------------------------------------------

LEARNING PIPELINE

Observation
↓

Evidence
↓

Verification
↓

Lesson
↓

Knowledge Update
↓

Playbook Update
↓

Wisdom Entry
↓

Future Recommendation

--------------------------------------------------

WHAT SHOULD BE LEARNED

Capture:

- Better workflows
- Better material choices
- Better pricing
- Better estimates
- Better communication
- Better quality methods
- Repeated customer requests
- Common mistakes

--------------------------------------------------

VERIFY BEFORE LEARNING

Before creating permanent knowledge ask:

Is there evidence?

Has this happened more than once?

Does it improve future decisions?

Can it be explained clearly?

If not, store it as a temporary observation.

--------------------------------------------------

KNOWLEDGE PROMOTION

New information moves through stages:

Observation

↓

Candidate Lesson

↓

Reviewed Lesson

↓

Approved Knowledge

↓

Wisdom

Only reviewed knowledge becomes part of the permanent AI Brain.

--------------------------------------------------

AI RESPONSIBILITIES

Continuously identify:

Repeated success

Repeated failure

Knowledge gaps

Outdated documents

Playbooks requiring updates

Duplicate information

--------------------------------------------------

QUALITY METRICS

The Learning Engine succeeds when:

Repeated mistakes decrease.

Playbooks improve.

Project estimates become more accurate.

Knowledge becomes easier to find.

The company becomes more consistent.

--------------------------------------------------

FINAL PRINCIPLE

Experience has value only when it is captured, verified and shared.

Every completed project should leave the Wood-Booster AI Brain wiser than before.
