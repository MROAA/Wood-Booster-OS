WOOD-BOOSTER RISK ANALYSIS

Purpose

Every project contains uncertainty.

The purpose of risk analysis is not to eliminate all risk.

Its purpose is to recognize important risks early enough that informed
decisions can be made.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

CORE PRINCIPLES

Every significant decision should consider:

-   Probability
-   Impact
-   Detectability
-   Prevention
-   Recovery

Risk ignored becomes surprise.

Risk understood becomes planning.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

WHEN TO PERFORM RISK ANALYSIS

Always perform a review before:

-   Accepting a custom project
-   Ordering expensive materials
-   Starting production
-   Introducing new tools
-   Testing new techniques
-   Making strategic business decisions

------------------------------------------------------------------------

RISK CATEGORIES

Evaluate risks related to:

Customer

Materials

Suppliers

Workshop

Machines

Quality

Schedule

Budget

Safety

Weather

Technology

Knowledge

------------------------------------------------------------------------

RISK MATRIX

Estimate:

Probability 1 = Rare 2 = Unlikely 3 = Possible 4 = Likely 5 = Almost
certain

Impact 1 = Negligible 2 = Minor 3 = Moderate 4 = Serious 5 = Critical

Prioritize risks with both high probability and high impact.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

AI RESPONSIBILITIES

For each identified risk the AI should provide:

Description

Possible cause

Likelihood

Potential impact

Preventive action

Contingency plan

Responsible person

Review date

------------------------------------------------------------------------

AFTER EVERY PROJECT

Record:

Which risks occurred?

Which risks were avoided?

Were estimates accurate?

Should the Playbook change?

Should Wisdom be updated?

Can future projects reduce this risk?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

FINAL PRINCIPLE

Do not fear risk.

Understand it.

Prepare for it.

Learn from it.

Well-managed risk protects quality, customers and the future of
Wood-Booster.
