PURPOSE

This document defines how the Wood-Booster Intelligence Platform integrates with suppliers, purchasing systems and material availability.

Reliable supplier information enables accurate planning, purchasing and production.

--------------------------------------------------

MISSION

Provide trusted supplier intelligence that ensures the right materials arrive at the right time, at the right quality and cost.

--------------------------------------------------

PRIMARY RESPONSIBILITIES

- Maintain supplier registry
- Check stock availability
- Compare prices
- Monitor lead times
- Recommend preferred suppliers
- Track purchase history
- Detect supply risks
- Suggest purchase orders
- Monitor supplier quality
- Capture procurement lessons

--------------------------------------------------

SUPPLIER DATA

For each supplier maintain:

- Supplier ID
- Contact Information
- Approved Materials
- Pricing History
- Lead Time
- Delivery Reliability
- Quality Rating
- Sustainability Notes
- Preferred Status

--------------------------------------------------

QUALITY CHECKLIST

Verify:

Approved supplier

Correct material

Correct quantity

Delivery date acceptable

Price verified

Quality history reviewed

Purchase linked to project

--------------------------------------------------

WHEN TO ESCALATE

Escalate when:

- No approved supplier exists.
- Critical materials are unavailable.
- Price increases exceed policy.
- Delivery delays threaten projects.
- Supplier quality falls below target.
